On the money: ₹9.99 crore is the recommendation — the top 249 cells by cooling per rupee, under a ₹10 crore cap. Treating all 4,157 treatable cells would cost ₹167.5 crore, which is an upper bound nothing here proposes. Likewise the two cooling figures: −0.99 °C is averaged over the cells actually treated, −0.51 °C over the whole 8,144-cell grid including the 3,987 that are water, existing tree cover or low priority and are never touched.
