Urban Heat Island Mitigation · Guwahati

From satellite radiance to a costed cooling plan

Four modules, one shared data contract. Landsat thermal imagery is reduced to a 100 m grid, a regression model predicts surface temperature, quantile tiering ranks priority, and a rule-based engine attaches an intervention and a cost to every cell the dashboard draws.
